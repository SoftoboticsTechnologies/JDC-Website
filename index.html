Where we're going. Why we go.
Mission

To build spaces that are worthy of the life lived inside them.

The mission is simple because it has to be. It needs to survive a boardroom and a construction site. “Worthy of the lives inside”: that's the question every JDC project is held against, from foundation to finish.

Vision

To be the builder that people point to, not when they move in, but a decade later.

When the building has become a part of their story. The vision is long. JDC is not building for the market cycle. It's building for the generation that will inherit what we make, and will judge us not on what we said, but on what we left behind.
