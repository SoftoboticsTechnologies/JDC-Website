01 — Logo family
01 Logo · The family

Six keystones, one family.

Two courses of three, the upper course stepped by one unit, as a mason would lay them: a mark built, not drawn. Every lockup is set on that unit, x. Always use the master artwork; never retype a lockup.

See the city through the mark.

The campaign poster, translated to the web. One message, open sky for type, and a giant mark that reads like glass over the skyline.

15 — Motion

Every move is set, not thrown.

16 — Accessibility & build
16 Accessibility & build

Built for everyone.

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. Every colour, size and motion rule above already meets it; these are the behaviours that complete it.

Skip link to main content, first in tab order. Landmarks: header, nav, main, footer; one H1 per page. Focus ring 2 px, 4 px offset: Obsidian on light, Amber on dark. Overlays trap focus; Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger. Contrast 4.5:1 body, 3:1 for 24 px+ and UI. No small text on Terracotta or Concrete Grey. Targets 44 × 44 px minimum; body text never below 16 px. Alt text on every image; renders say Artist's impression. Performance: preload hero and font, lazy-load the rest, font-display: swap, no layout shift.
